# ``DiscordGameServices``

Compose published backend account, lobby and profile operations with explicit consent and ownership.

## Overview

DiscordGameServices adds provisional/publisher backend account descriptors and snapshot conveniences over the existing typed REST catalog. It does not embed a native Discord runtime, guess undocumented wire protocols or supply browser/storage UI. Construction starts no requests, synchronization or account migration.

Read <doc:BackendAccountLinking> before provisioning or linking identities, and <doc:LobbyAndProfileWorkflows> to reduce unchanged writes without changing replacement semantics.
